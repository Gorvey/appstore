## Introduction

**Reactive Resume** is a free and open-source resume builder for creating, updating, sharing, and exporting resumes, with support for self-hosted deployments.

## Features

- **Resume Building**: Supports real-time previews, drag-and-drop ordering, custom sections, and rich text editing.
- **Templates and Export**: Includes multiple templates, PDF and JSON export, and customization for page size, colors, fonts, and spacing.
- **Privacy and Control**: Can be self-hosted, has no tracking or ads by default, and lets users control their own data.
